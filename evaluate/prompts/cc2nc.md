# Task: Theorem Elicitation

`input/core_claim.md` contains a short informal claim describing the central result of a
research paper. Turn it into a precise natural-language theorem statement.

Deliverable: write the statement to `output/nl_claim.md`.

Requirements:

- Read the input with your own tools. Never modify anything under `input/`.
- State one theorem: quantify every object, name every hypothesis explicitly, and give the
  exact conclusion. A reader must be able to formalize it without access to the paper.
- Keep it self-contained: define or characterize every non-standard notion you use.
- Write the statement only. Do not include a proof, a proof sketch, or commentary.
- English only. Mathematical notation is allowed.
- Finish only after `output/nl_claim.md` exists and contains the final statement.
